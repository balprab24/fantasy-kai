# League plan S2 — rest-of-season projections, lineup optimizer, team needs, 2026-10-30

Branch `feat/leagues-s2`, stacked on `feat/leagues-s1` (PR #76, draft). Built and reviewed by
Claude Code against Part B S2 of `docs/research/2026-10-28-leagues-trades-plan.md` and the
approved S2 plan.

## What was built

- **`scoring/ExplainedScore`.** The explained number the invariant table names: parts
  (stat × rate) plus the bonus remainder. The total *is* `ScoringEngine.score`, so the number and
  its explanation cannot disagree. `ProjectionController` now uses it, with an unchanged wire
  shape (`parts`, `bonusPoints`), pinned by a new assertion.
- **`JdbcProjectionInputs.histories(target, playerIds)`.** The job's history read, filtered by
  player through the stats primary key: 200 players in about 30 ms locally, instead of two
  sequential scans. Both paths share one builder, and a test shows they agree.
- **`leagues/RestOfSeason`.** Each rostered player's expected points for every week before the
  league's playoffs, under the league's ruleset:
  - 0 on a bye;
  - 0 in the first week when Out;
  - 0 every week on a reserve list;
  - Questionable and Doubtful play.

  The first remaining week comes from `games` only. Nothing is stored, and the projection job
  stays off.
- **`leagues/LineupOptimizer`.** An exact assignment (Hungarian). K and DST are listed, unfilled,
  at an equal 0 for every team.
- **`leagues/TeamNeeds`, `GET /api/v1/leagues/{id}/needs`.** Every team's best lineup for every
  remaining week; each starting slot averaged; the member's team against the league median.
  - It returns 404 for another member's league, and 409 for a league without a ruleset.
  - IDP slots are reported as not modelled.
- **`leagues/ProjectionUncertainty`.** σ = a + b·μ per position, fitted by a new
  `backtest.sh uncertainty` stage and recorded in `docs/perf/projection-accuracy.md`. S3 will
  read it.
- **Frontend.**
  - A "Team needs" panel on the league page: one row per slot, the starter, mine, the league
    median, and a signed difference. A surplus is in the one quality green; a deficit stays ink
    with a true minus.
  - Each roster row shows its expected points a week.
  - Choosing a team or syncing re-fetches the needs.

## The σ fit, measured

`./scripts/backtest.sh uncertainty`:
- **Data:** production `weighted-average-v1`, population P, 2021–2025, 19,025 player-weeks.
- **Half PPR fit (the constants):** QB 6.61 + 0.086μ, RB 4.03 + 0.273μ, WR 3.50 + 0.319μ,
  TE 3.14 + 0.331μ.
- **Sanity gate (A7's shape):** passed.
- **Robustness under PPR and 0 PPR:** σ at μ = 10 moves by at most 0.3 points.

## What I did not fix, and why

- **The roster rows' slot column says "Bench" for everyone in the seeded league.** That is the
  seed's doing: a real import carries Sleeper's starters. The optimizer ignores listed slots
  either way; it picks the best lineup.
- **The flat line.** The weighted average gives the same line every week, apart from byes and
  absences. Opponent, weather and game script are not modelled. That is the projection's
  documented scope, and the error σ quantifies it.
- **Reserve means out for the season.** A player on IR projects 0 for every remaining week until
  he is activated. Stated in the code; return dates are not modelled.
- **A bonus on an expected line is all-or-nothing at the mean.** This matches the existing
  projection read; expected bonuses (research `BonusOdds`) are out of scope.
- **One σ fit for every ruleset.** It is robust across the three presets (±0.3 at μ = 10). A
  league with very different scoring is an approximation that S3's calibration gate will test.
- **K and DST are 0 for everyone.** They cancel out of every comparison here. Their variance,
  which matters for win probability, is S3's.
- **The first remaining week comes from kickoffs, not stats.** Between Monday night and the
  Tuesday pull it can be a day ahead of `TargetWeekResolver`; documented.
- **The real-league check is still open**; it needs the owner's Sleeper username.

## The argument against merging as written

The panel puts precise-looking numbers ("−1.8 a week") on a projection whose own error is 6–8
points a game. A member could read a 0.3 surplus as meaningful when it is well inside the noise.
The panel says these are expectations, but it does not show the uncertainty that S2 itself
measured. A stronger version would grey out differences smaller than, say, a quarter of σ, or
print the σ beside each row.

What would prove the readout useful or not: whether S3's calibration test finds that these
expectations, with this σ, actually predict head-to-head results.
