## What was done, in order

1. **Pre-flight** on the VM: HEAD `b752919`, working tree clean, disk 34 GB free, image `96f053dc…`,
   Caddyfile `8470f09b…`, `origin/main` visible.
2. **§7 step 1 (prove the rollback against a newer schema) was not applicable**: it is for a change that
   carries a migration, and V1–V11 are unchanged. Rolling back is the image tag.
3. **Backup**, copy off, **restore and compare** (above).
4. **Tag the running image** as the rollback.
5. **Pull**: `git status --short` printed nothing, `git pull --ff-only origin main`, and HEAD was asserted equal
   to the approved SHA before anything was built.
6. **Build without swapping** (`docker compose build backend`), then inspect the **new** image before it replaced
   anything: `bcprov-jdk18on-1.87`, `bucket4j_jdk17-{core,lettuce,redis-common}-8.22.0`,
   `tomcat-embed-core-10.1.59`. The bumps took; they were not just declared.
7. **Swap** (`up -d --wait backend`), guarded to roll back to the tagged image if the new container was not
   healthy. It was healthy; the guard did not fire. Application start 10.3 s.
8. **Caddy**: nothing to do (unchanged); **§7 step 7 (ingest once)**: nothing to do (no new columns).
